# Colony Summary

Adds a Summary tab that shows every colonist on one row of a vanilla-style table: age, the fight-or-flee setting (changeable right there), bleeding with time until death, conditions that need tending, every skill with its passion, weapon, worn apparel, overall sharp, blunt and heat armor, comfortable temperature range and mass carried. Hover over anything for details, click an item to open its info card, and click a column header to sort by it. Colonists in caravans and transporters are listed too.
