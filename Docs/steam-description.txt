[b]Adds a Summary tab that shows every colonist on one row: health, skills, gear and protection.[/b]

See at a glance who's bleeding out, who needs tending, who's best at what and who's under-armored, without clicking through each colonist's tabs. It looks and works like the vanilla Assign and Animals tabs.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Age:[/b] Each colonist's biological age. Hover for the full age.
[*][b]Fight or flee:[/b] The vanilla hostility response button, changeable right in the table.
[*][b]Bleeding:[/b] Time until death by blood loss in red, or the bleed rate when there's no risk of bleeding out soon.
[*][b]Tending:[/b] How many conditions need tending now. Hover to see which.
[*][b]Skills:[/b] Every skill, in the Bio tab's order, with level, passion and the Bio tab's tooltip. Skills added by other mods get their own columns.
[*][b]Gear:[/b] Weapon and worn apparel as icons. Damaged items show a condition bar, in red below half. Hover for name, quality and condition, and click to open the info card.
[*][b]Protection:[/b] Overall sharp, blunt and heat armor, calculated the same way as the Gear tab.
[*][b]Comfort:[/b] Each colonist's comfortable temperature range.
[*][b]Sorting:[/b] Click a column header to sort by it. Click a colonist's name to jump to them.
[*][b]Everyone:[/b] Colonists in caravans and transporters are listed too.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
